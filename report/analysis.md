## 2. Market structure: what the live data says

Basis for everything in this section: quant/quant_summary.md and quant/price_table.csv (SMP500 settled AH sales: last 15 per item plus 24 h aggregates; LootSeller daily candles of the top /orders bid and cheapest /ah listing; jpsoftware last sale), all pulled 2026-08-29; your log 2026-08-26..29.

### 2.1 The AH pays per stack, and the stack price barely depends on the item

Full-stack clearing prices per unit on 2026-08-29 (last-15 stack median): cobblestone 155 (9.9K per stack), sand 313 (20K), spruce planks 156 (10K), gravel 430 (27.5K), deepslate 453 (29K), stone 547 (35K), torch 547 (35K), dirt 570 (36K), glass 688 (44K), chest 781 (50K), bone 703 (45K). A stack of almost anything basic is a 10-50K listing. The consequence: a method's value is (stacks you can list per day) x (price per stack), and the first factor is capped by 18 slots and your clicking, so the only ways up are higher-value forms per slot (beds 20-90K each, concrete stacks 190-400K, map stacks 445K, key singles 540K) or zero-slot exits (/orders deliveries).

### 2.2 /orders fill at a fraction of the AH stack price

Top /orders bid vs AH stack median, 2026-08-29: spruce log 72 vs 328 (p25 192), bone 137 vs 703 (p25 625), obsidian 601 vs 1,094, TNT 1,700 vs 3,906, blackstone 850 vs 3,125 (24 h average 1,463), soul sand 85 vs 781, map 3,900 vs 6,953, emerald block 21,100 vs 27,969, iron block 2,500 vs 4,688, coal block 1,600 vs 7,031 (p25 3,140), lapis block 700 vs 1,875, ghast tear 10,000 vs 15,600. Two readings of the same fact:

- As a buyer, orders are the cheap input channel (that is your Ghast Tear trade).
- As a seller, orders are the fast, zero-slot, probably untaxed exit, at 30-80% of the AH price. Useful for totems (103K bid vs 107K floor), ancient debris (1.3M vs 1.5M), EGap (1.5M vs 1.59M), heavy core (3.5M vs 3.9M), maps (3,900 as a floor exit).

Fill speed on the order side is the number nobody measures. June-25 fill rates on deep books were 38-64%; anecdotes run from instant to 24 h. Orders open ~24 h, 90 h to claim, refundable if cancelled unfilled (fandom Money page; Voidrik 2026-08-27 got ~8M back on a cancelled turtle-shell order). What happens to deliveries not claimed inside 90 h is undocumented.

### 2.3 Slot economics

18 /ah + 18 /orders slots on default rank since the June 2-3 2026 update (was 25; Donut+ 45 for $5.49/month, Donut++ 90). One listing per slot; non-stackables (potions, books, tools, beds, minecarts, shard tools) are one item per slot. Your own log: 53 floor-priced listings matched to "bought your" lines sold in a median 1.4 min, p75 9.5 min; above-floor lots (ghast tear 76.6K single, TNT at 3,333) sat 29-30 h. So at the floor, selling is not the bottleneck; order fills and your listing labour are. One open contradiction: a replay of your listed/sold pairs shows 28 listings open simultaneously at 03:17:23 on 08-29, which does not fit an 18-slot cap. Check the GUI (section 5).

### 2.4 The three channels

- /ah: player listings, sort by lowest price / recently listed, Quick Buy Confirmation toggle in settings. AH purchase history was removed 2026-06-05, so keep your own clearing-price sheet.
- /orders: player buy orders, bulk, cheap; you are paid when you deliver into someone's order. One order can cover thousands of units (Bubsy's 1M-log order), so order slots are for depth, AH slots for throughput.
- /sell: server fixed base price, /worth <item> shows it. Known base prices: spruce slab 12, dried kelp block 300, bone meal ~30, pink petal and wildflower ~10. Everything else unknown. Since the multiplier was removed (2026-06-02) the /sell channel only pays at mega scale with a Sell Axe (3,000 shards or ~24M on /ah for 18 h left, Bubsy 2026-08-28; self-destructs 24 h after the original purchase). Bubsy's own slab farm nets ~22-24 per log at a 72-74 log price, which is 1.09M logs per day just to pay for a 24M axe (M17).

### 2.5 The tax is unknown

No source states an AH tax. Evidence for ~0% on the claim side: "You earned $6.4M from auction" on 2026-08-28 19:49:48 equals exactly 2 x 3.2M Flow trim listings; "$450K earned while away" on 08-27 equals 3 terracotta at 100K + 45 TNT at 3,333 exactly. A fee charged at listing time would not show in those lines. quant/tax_probe.py on the log gave ratios 0.03-25.7 because Bedrock buyers (22% of your sales, names starting with ".") are dropped by the parser, so it proves nothing. Every card carries a 10% sensitivity. At 10%, these die: dragon head, elytra, netherite ingot and block flips (1.4-7% spreads), netherite block to 9 ingots, at-top diamond block flip, order-to-AH head flip, mojang banner pattern, all AH-to-AH compression loops. Everything above a ~15% spread survives.

### 2.6 Which price to believe

- Single-item prices are polluted by money transfers routed through the AH: chest single 40,000 with a 24 h max of 25.9B, stick single 10,000 (1 d average 166,796), water bucket 25K median vs your 11 buys at 1.8-10K, lava bucket 90K flat vs 5-17K in your log. Price off last-15 full-stack medians, or for concrete off the 24 h volume-weighted average, never off "1d avg" or single sales.
- Last-15 medians can be one seller's wall: blackstone 3,125 (15 identical sales), white bed 20,000 (15/15), flow trim 100,000 (15/15), map 6,953 (4 identical stack sales). A wall is a real price until you add supply to it.
- SMP500 "1h" and "1d" trade counts are mislabeled (ratio ~0.23-0.26, not 1/24); treat trades/day as relative liquidity, not absolute.
- The June-25 order snapshot is two months stale and post-/shop-removal by three weeks. Block bids moved 4-5x since then (emerald block 5,000 to 21,100; gold block 4,300 to 20,500; ghast tear 140 to 10,000), so every craft margin built on a June unit bid (paper 103, iron 170, gold 600, string 72, glass 280, wool 103, crying obsidian 201, glowstone 430) is fiction until you see the bid yourself.
- LootSeller is fresh only for popular items. Tall/short dry grass, leaf litter, bush and creaking heart bids are dated 2026-07-28; spruce log 2026-08-08..11; diamond AH floor 08-12.

### 2.8 Rules and mechanics that bind

No macros, scripts, autoclickers, freecam, inventory mods, crafting mods, ESP/xray; max 5 accounts; no duplicating items (TNT dupers are unaddressed and therefore a risk). Vanilla toggle-sneak/attack/use in Controls is a setting, not a macro. Block limits ~20,000 per type (hoppers, dispensers, barrels), chests unlimited. Shards are real-money only plus 1 per 10 min online (87 lines in your log at exact 10-minute cadence = 6/h). Shard tools self-destruct 24 h after the ORIGINAL purchase, so an /ah drill has less than 24 h left the moment you buy it. Spawners cannot be listed on /ah or /orders, trade OTC only, and are "pie-chartable" (fandom Spawner page). World border 225K; expansion to 30M expected within ~2 years (W1zox, July 2026); finite items (elytra, dragon head, netherite, debris, gilded, deepslate emerald ore) are on the wiki's sell-before list.
