01

Evidence types matter.

Asks are offers; bids have finite depth; completed sales are observations. Historical item variants may not match modern ones.

02

Quantity changes price.

The next buyer might bid less. Selling a whole chest is not equivalent to selling one item at the best visible order.

03

Costs decide profit.

Materials, holding time, sale charges and an expiring Sell Axe can turn attractive revenue into a loss.
